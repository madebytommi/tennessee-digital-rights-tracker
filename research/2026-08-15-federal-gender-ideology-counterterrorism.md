## Provisional Hypothesis

> Official federal executive branch policy established a sequential progression from administrative definitions of biological sex in January 2025 to federal grant/funding conditions in mid-2025, culminating in the integration of gender-related ideological classifications into domestic terrorism directives (NSPM-7) and national counterterrorism strategy (May 2026) targeted at violent secular political groups. However, the available primary evidence does not establish that peaceful transgender advocacy is classified as terrorism, nor does it establish active state-level counterterrorism operations under this framework in Tennessee.

This is not a conclusion. Evidence may confirm, narrow, qualify, contradict, or leave it unresolved.
