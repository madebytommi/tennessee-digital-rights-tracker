# Tracker Research Notes

Use this template as a working evidence record for a substantive investigation. It operationalizes `docs/research-protocol.md`; that protocol and `docs/methodology.md` remain authoritative.

Research notes are not automatically publication-ready. Files under `_entries/` are public Tracker entries, and findings must satisfy `docs/research-protocol.md` before they are converted into an entry using `templates/ENTRY_TEMPLATE.md`. AI-generated text is not evidence.

Do not collect or unnecessarily reproduce sensitive personal information. Remove unused prompts and add rows as needed.
